❓ How to Play

  1. Cast — tap the big button (or anywhere on the water).
  2. When the bobber dips and you see !, tap to hook the fish.
  3. Hold to reel in and let go to give line. Keep the gauge's needle inside the striped green zone until the fish is landed.
  4. Your catch goes in the 🧺 haul. When it's full, sell it for cash.
  5. Spend cash in the 🛒 Shop on better gear, new bait and new places to fish.
  6. Hire helpers and buy boats in 🏢 Business — they earn money even while the game is closed.
  7. Every new catch goes in the 📖 Journal. Can you find all of them?
