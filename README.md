# Fishing Tycoon 🎣

An iPad-first incremental fishing game. You start with a cheap rod, a tin
tackle box, $25 and a little pond. Cast, catch, sell your haul, upgrade your
gear, discover strange things, hire a crew, buy boats — and slowly build a
surprisingly successful fishing business.

**Play it:** https://rvenning.github.io/fishing-tycoon/

## The game

- **Fishing is a few seconds of real involvement.** Cast, wait for the bobber
  to dip, tap when you see the **!**, then hold to reel and let go to give line —
  keeping the marker in a striped green zone that wanders as the fish swims.
  Stronger fish and heavier-than-your-line fish fight harder. There's an
  **Easy reeling** setting for younger players.
- **56 catches across 6 locations** — Little Pond, River Bend, Crystal Lake,
  Coastal Pier, Open Sea and the Deep Sea. Five rarities, variable sizes,
  personal bests, golden fish worth five times as much, and oddities: an old
  boot, a rubber duck, a message in a bottle, treasure, fossils, a glowing relic.
- **The Fishing Journal** shows silhouettes of everything you haven't found,
  with a one-line personality for everything you have, plus a records page.
- **Gear:** 6 rods, 5 reels, 5 lines, 6 tackle boxes and 6 kinds of bait
  (including Magnet Bait, which fish ignore and boots love).
- **The business:** six helpers (Sam, Mia, Penny, Old Jack, Nana Flo and Skipper
  Kofi), four boats, and a harbour of buildings — dock, bait shop, fish market,
  cold storage, charter business, a public aquarium that earns for every
  species in your journal, and finally the Harbour HQ. The harbour on the
  Business screen fills in as you buy.
- **Passive and offline income,** capped at a few hours away (longer with Nana
  Flo and cold storage). Active fishing always stays the bigger earner.
- **44 goals** with rewards, and **events** that turn up while you fish: Golden
  Hour, Storm, Lucky Day, the Mysterious Ripple, the Fishing Competition and a
  Huge Shadow.
- Synthesized sound effects and a gentle tune per location, with separate
  music/effects volume, mute, and reduced motion.

Aimed at Rosalie (and up) — readable for an 8-year-old, with enough economy to
keep an adult casting. A steady player reaches the Deep Sea in about four hours
of fishing and the Harbour HQ in about twelve.

## Built on gamekit

Profiles with PINs, family sync across devices, the sound engine, screens and
modals, and PWA install all come from the shared
[gamekit](https://github.com/rvenning/gamekit) library, vendored into `lib/`.

```bash
node "../gamekit/tools/sync-to-game.js" "../fishing-tycoon"
```

Never edit `lib/` directly — the next sync overwrites it.

## Layout

```
js/catches.js    the catalogue: every fish and oddity, sizes, values, art recipes
js/locations.js  the six fishing spots
js/shop.js       everything buyable, as tracks of levels with effects
js/goals.js      goals and rewards
js/events.js     timed events and competition medals
js/game.js       Econ — every rule, DOM-free, dice injected
js/reel.js       the reeling mini-game as a pure simulation (+ a test brain)
js/fishing.js    one cast as a state machine
js/storage.js    gamekit storage + merge
js/audio.js      sound effects and music
js/art.js        every fish, person and boat, painted with canvas paths
js/scene.js      the fishing scene and the harbour
js/ui.js         shop, journal, business, goals, map, haul
js/main.js       App: input, frame loop, saving, events, offline, tutorial
```

## PWA

`manifest.json`, `sw.js` (network-first) and `icons/` (generated by
`node tools/make-icons.js`). Bump the version before each deploy:
`node lib/tools/stamp-version.js . --bump`.

## Local development

```bash
npx http-server . -p 8132 -c-1
```

Tests (no dependencies): `npm test`, i.e. `node --test tests/*.test.js` —
content lint, rules, save/merge and balance bots. `node tools/timeline.js`
prints when a steady bot reaches each milestone.

## Storage

localStorage prefix `ft_`, Firestore collection `fishingtycoon` in the shared
family project. Money is stored as earned/spent counters so a sync merge can
never resurrect spent cash. The Firebase API key in `js/firebase-config.js` is a
public client key restricted to Firestore — safe to commit.
